The portable graphics runtime downloads PySide6 (Qt for Python, LGPLv3/GPLv3/commercial) and its dependencies.
See https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html for license terms and notices.
Installers use PyInstaller for native Windows packaging; its bootloader has a distribution exception.
Pong includes raylib source under its zlib license at vendor/raylib/LICENSE, and third-party notices in that source tree.
Dependencies retain their original licenses; generated installations include upstream notices.
